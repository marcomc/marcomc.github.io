## A small site, with a light footprint

This website presents software development, web development and DevOps consulting services provided directly by Marco Massari Calderone under the name MarcoMC. Services are scoped and quoted individually. For consulting enquiries and project quotes: [marco@marcomc.com](mailto:marco@marcomc.com).

There are no advertising trackers, contact forms or embedded third-party scripts. Your chosen appearance is stored locally in your browser. Selecting “Seasonal” saves a preference to change the appearance with the seasons.

GoatCounter provides aggregate visitor statistics, including visited pages, referring websites and approximate countries. It uses no analytics cookies. Page query strings and fragments are excluded; referrals include only the website origin. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable both counting and the automatic counter request. Automated browsers and local previews are not counted. Visit counts are estimates and the public total may be cached for up to four hours. Details: [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy).

The hosting and network providers process connection data to deliver and protect the site. If you email me, I use the information you provide to answer your enquiry and discuss the requested services. Please do not include sensitive information in an initial enquiry.
