Software is only part of the story.

My work moves between applications and the systems that run them. I’m as interested in a clear interface as I am in a reliable deployment, a well-designed API or a script that saves someone an hour every week.

I work across Swift, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, PHP and shell scripting, with a practical foundation in Linux, containers, infrastructure automation and SQL. I choose tools to suit the problem and leave decisions and systems understandable.

Outside the screen, I’m drawn to the open road and the world underwater. Motorcycling and diving make room for exploration, patience and attention to detail. Back at home, that same curiosity finds its way into Raspberry Pi projects and connected devices.
